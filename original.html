How to Play

Carve designs, craft mazes, or test them on foot!

Driving Controls:
  • W / Up : Accelerate forward
  • S / Down : Reverse / brake
  • A / D / Left / Right : Steer wheels
  • H : Honk tractor horn
  • Mobile Touch : Drag anywhere on the screen for the virtual joystick!
Creative Tools:
  • Width (1x / 3x): Toggle between standard mower and giant combine harvester cutter.
  • Auto-Maze: Instantly carves out a labyrinth maze with entrance & exit.
  • Maze Runner: Spawn a visitor character to physically walk through and solve your custom maze!
  • Export Art: Take a high-resolution bird's-eye photo of your farm masterpiece.
