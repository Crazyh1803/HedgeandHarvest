**Keyboard rules**
- Enter or Space on a summary toggles the menu natively, and Tab moves between the buttons inside.
- Opening a menu closes the other one and clears `ui.held`. Pressing a summary also clears `ui.held`.
- Escape closes an open menu and focuses its summary. It only does this when no modal is open.
- A click outside, or Tab moving focus out of an open menu, closes it.
- Clicking any button in a menu panel closes the menu and focuses the visible summary before the button's own handler runs. So the modal's saved focus is the summary, and `exportPng` leaves focus there too.
- Share-from-win (`#win-share`) is unchanged.
- Any key pressed while focus is inside a `.menu`, including its summary, is ignored by the global handler. Arrows, WASD, U, H, M and R do not move or act on the tractor or runner.
- This only covers keys inside the menus. Arrows pressed while a Carve/Run button has focus now behave as before.
