**Hooks**
- `__cornfield.animation()` returns `{phase, moving, amount}`.
- `__cornfield.minimap()` returns:
  - `rect`: viewport in cell units, unclamped.
  - `visible`: the same rect clamped to the field.
  - `actor`: `{kind: 'tractor'|'runner', x, y}`.
  - `field`: `{w, h, cell, pad, canvasW, canvasH}`.
  - `close` and `tab`.
- `view()` now also reports `tab`.
- `CornfieldAudio.patternAt(step)` is a pure function returning `[{voice, freq, dur, gain}]` over a 32-step loop. `CornfieldAudio.bpm` is 118.

**IDs**
- `#minimap` is the canvas and `#minimap-note` is its caption. The minimap card sits above the tabs in `.panel`, so it stays visible in close-up whichever tab is open.
- `#sidebar-tabs` is the tablist, with `#tab-play` / `#panel-play` and `#tab-settings` / `#panel-settings`. Play is the default. Size, season and runner moved into Settings with their IDs unchanged.

**Behaviour**
- **Minimap:**
  - A 1 px-per-cell base is rebuilt only when the layer is rebuilt (carve, season, undo, resize).
  - Gates, the viewport box and the actor dot are drawn live every frame.
  - The backing store changes only when the field size changes. `html` has `scrollbar-gutter: stable`, so tab switches don't change the stage width.
- **Tabs:**
  - Arrow/Home/End keys on the tablist are handled there and never reach the actor.
  - The global key handler also ignores keys from the tablist.
- **Gait:**
  - The phase advances by distance travelled. It eases to neutral and snaps still at rest.
  - It resets on teleports and stays neutral in reduced motion.
  - Blocked moves don't change the gait; the existing bump jig is unchanged.
  - Icons and PNG export call `drawAnimal` with no pose, so they stay static.
- **Music:**
  - The slow random pads are replaced by a deterministic C-major I–V–vi–IV loop with add9 colour: kick, snare, eighth hats, bouncy bass, a square-wave lead and short chord stabs.
  - Scheduling uses a 0.15 s lookahead on a 30 ms interval.
  - One shared noise buffer is reused.
  - Every music voice is tracked, and turning music off cancels queued notes and cuts ringing ones.
  - The lowpass cutoff went from 2400 to 7000 Hz.
